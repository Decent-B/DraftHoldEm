04

Secret draft

Pick order is earned, not random.

High bid first

Initial bid groups stay ordered from highest to lowest.

Tied paid bids re-bid once

Resolve the highest tied group, then the next one.

The re-bid is spent

If it ties, position decides—no third bid.

06

Fold is not all-in

Only one of them keeps drafting.

FOLD

Your hand ends

A♠7♥
  • No more drafts
  • No more betting
  • Cannot win a pot
ALL-IN

Your hand continues

K♠Q♠◆
  • Still bids and drafts
  • No more betting
  • Eligible for showdown
Side pots are automatic. You can only win the pots covered by your committed chips.
